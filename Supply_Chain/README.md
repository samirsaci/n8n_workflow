# 🚛 Automation for Logistics and Supply Chain Operations — n8n Workflows

This folder contains all workflows dedicated to **logistics automation**, **operational efficiency**, and **supply chain analytics** using **n8n**.

These templates help automate repetitive supply chain tasks such as:

- Optimizing transportation and routing
- Calculating CO₂ emissions for shipments and business travel
- Processing logistics orders and operational emails
- Performing geocoding and distance estimation
- Automating ABC analysis and operational reporting
- Connecting Gmail, Google Sheets, APIs, and AI models for logistics insights

All workflows are:

- **Ready to deploy** in n8n (JSON included)
- **Documented** with their own README
- **Modular and customizable**
- Most of them include **YouTube tutorials** for step-by-step guidance
